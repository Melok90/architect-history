## Brand Name
Pulse

## What is it all about?
A mobile fintech wallet with a personal AI assistant that unifies banking (IBAN, card, transfers, payments) and crypto (buy, sell, hold USDC/BTC/ETH) in one interface. Every morning it tells you exactly how much you can safely spend today, and every financial decision is made through conversation with the AI instead of navigating dashboards.

## Target Audience
People aged 20–40 who already use neobanks daily, get paid by salary, pay for purchases and make transfers. They hold some crypto (stablecoins or a bit of BTC/ETH on an exchange) but are not traders and don't want to deal with jargon, seed phrases or complex interfaces. Financially literate enough to understand basic tools, but they value clarity, trust and simplicity over features. Aesthetically they expect a calm, modern, mobile-first product that feels intelligent and premium — like the best neobanks — never cluttered, never aggressive, with the AI as a friendly expert that makes money feel effortless.

## Colors
Light, calm and confident: soft lavender-tinted whites and pale mauve grays as the base, pure white for cards and elevated surfaces, near-black graphite for text. A single vivid indigo-violet brand accent (#4648D4-class) used for primary actions and brand moments, with a muted emerald green reserved for success states. Light theme, no dark base.
